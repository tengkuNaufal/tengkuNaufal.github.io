Cogniva

Learn a topic by teaching it to an AI student that asks questions and holds misconceptions.

Users explain a concept on a whiteboard while speaking; the AI learner reacts to both what's drawn and what's said, then an evaluator agent reports what was wrong, missing, or confusing.

  • Architected the session state machine with cyclic teaching rounds, inter-agent data contracts, and REST/WebSocket APIs.
  • Grounded the design in learning science — protégé effect, SRL, ICAP — with a faculty supervisor.
  • Represented ITB at the Grand Final in Amsterdam with a live booth demo.

02Team member · 4-person team

Cek Dulu

“Check first” — an anti-hype agent for Indonesian retail investors.

Splits stock posts from TikTok & X into individual claims and verifies each against official Sectors market data. Math runs in code and verdicts come from a rule table, not the LLM — consistent and explainable.

  • Accepts text, screenshots (OCR), video links, TikTok captions, and the mobile share sheet.
  • Verifies seven claim types and flags numbers that are correct but misleading.
  • Streamed progress, check history with status-change detection, private via Supabase RLS.

Hirup

Choose the route that's healthiest to breathe — not just the fastest.

A Jakarta multimodal trip planner that ranks routes by estimated inhaled PM2.5 dose. A LightGBM model estimates air quality along each route and combines it with travel mode and duration.

  • Flutter mobile app backed by a FastAPI service and Python ML data pipelines.
  • Routing via OpenTripPlanner 2 (public transport) and OSRM (road network).
  • Deployed with Docker Compose, Caddy, and GitHub Actions CI/CD on an Azure VM.

04Team member · 3-person team

MangroveMorph

Evidence-based screening for coastal mangrove protection on small islands.

Runs entirely in the browser: pick or draw a coastline, estimate wave reduction for different belt designs, find Pareto-optimal options, compare against a submerged breakwater, and estimate planting cost — returning ranges, not false precision.

  • Multi-objective optimization (belt width × stand age) → Pareto front, with multi-zone & budget caps.
  • Validated against published studies: 18/18 cases, backed by a Vitest suite.
  • Static, serverless deployment — nothing to maintain.

05Individual project

GitHub Trending ETL & Data Warehouse

Capturing short-lived daily trending data into a relational DB and a star-schema warehouse.

A two-stage scraper collects the daily list, enriches new repositories with topics, license, language composition, and releases, then loads them into a normalized PostgreSQL schema and a star-schema DW.

  • PKs, FKs, CHECK constraints, a trigger, and an M:N relationship with attributes.
  • Idempotent loader using unique grains + ON CONFLICT upserts.
  • Daily scheduling, robots.txt-respecting scraping, tuned with EXPLAIN ANALYZE.
